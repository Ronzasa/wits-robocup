## Implemented behaviour

- The deepest dangerous opposing forward receives a stable goal-side marker,
  with shot-threat memory and a handoff when the current marker cannot keep up.
  The keeper and ball chaser retain their responsibilities. In secure possession,
  the cover role also supplies the striker mark rather than adding another
  mandatory defender.
- At any current lead of **two goals or more**, all five players retreat into
  goal-defending slots. This takes priority over ordinary support, marking and
  pressing. They return to the balanced game immediately below a two-goal lead.
  The keeper continues shot tracking while the wall forms. Guard clearances
  preserve the assigned position, including at the edge of kicking range.
- An observed incoming opponent shot must precede a saved or reversed rebound.
  Only a winning arrival race and a clear, recoverable forward route can release
  two players. Three retain goal cover. Opponent control, a lost race, expiry or
  a goal cancels the counter. A generic loose ball cannot trigger it.
- Short receiver-led passes at 12/16 units per second augment the existing
  14/20/26 search. A forward third-player outlet can improve the pass's value;
  new short-link candidates penalise an immediate return pass. Routes still need a reachable receiver
  and interception margin. Short passing is a choice, not a forced sequence.
- Slot 4 is **Pelé**, with central finishing runs and extra reachable goal
  targets. Slot 2 is **Gerrard**, with a long-shot search that requires the ball
  to reach goal with useful remaining speed and a positive interception margin.
- Wall prediction coordinates a post-bounce collector and an outlet while
  preserving cover and the striker marker. A goal mouth is not treated as a
  reflecting wall. Existing receiver-aware side-wall and end-wall passes remain.
- Momentum-aware interception and early receiver movement improve effective
  pace. All engine attributes stay legal: player speed 8, kick impulse 22,
  ball speed cap 30, kick power at most 1. Names grant no attribute bonuses;
  outfielders gain no special goalkeeper powers.

## Development and selection

The initial 64-fixture comparison tested flat and staggered shields and
diagnostic feature removals. The staggered shape conceded substantially more,
so the complete flat-wall packages were calibrated. Range-edge movement,
counter cancellation and imaginary goal-mouth rebounds were fixed before the
final candidates. Every release candidate below enables all requested features.

Each played the same 240 native fixtures: 10 styles, 6 development seeds,
both sides and both kickoff arrangements. v7 scored **524**, with
