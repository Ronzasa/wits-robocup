# v6.1 local test record

Version: `6.1-pep-klopp-bounded-defense`.

The goalkeeper now attempts wide collections only when its estimated arrival
is within 1.10 seconds and at least 0.20 seconds ahead of the nearest opponent.
It also stops pursuing its own fast outgoing clearance on these wide routes.
Central collections and the existing predictive saves remain available.

The wall planner caches repeated direct-pass checks, skips identical launch
forecasts, and limits each search to 12 launch forecasts. Shared ball-race
forecasts remove duplicate work. Balanced counterpressing, quick counters,
coordinated cover, and receiver-aware wall passes remain enabled.

## Fresh evaluation

The policy was selected using development games and frozen before the fresh
evaluation. Each version played the same 624 fixtures: 13 fixed local opponent
styles, 12 unused seed blocks, both sides, and both kickoff arrangements.
The course engine was 0.1.0, configuration `372990895c7eca2a`, with 2,400 ticks
per match. Each version used one worker; two workers ran concurrently.

| Metric | v6.0 | v6.1 |
|---|---:|---:|
| Points | 1,465 | 1,473 |
| Wins / draws / losses | 460 / 85 / 79 | 462 / 87 / 75 |
| Goals scored | 1,657 | 1,610 |
| Goals conceded | 467 | 395 |
| Shots blocked | 1,265 | 1,470 |
| Completed passes | 8,293 | 8,320 |
| Mean decision time | 0.1065 ms | 0.0774 ms |

This is 15.4% fewer conceded goals, 16.2% more blocked shots, and 27.3% lower
mean decision time in these local games. Scoring fell by 2.8%.

The points-per-match change was +0.0128. A paired bootstrap over whole seed
blocks gave a 95% interval of **[-0.0545, +0.0801]**, using 5,000 resamples.
The overall points improvement is therefore inconclusive. These fixed opponents
do not establish a league rank or guarantee better results against the class.

There are matchup tradeoffs: the runner-overload style improved by 20 points
across its 48 fixtures, while the older v3.7 controller matchup lost 24 points.
Fast-counter fixtures lost three points; wall-runner fixtures gained two.

## Reliability and retained behavior

The official five-seed validator completed 2,000 decisions with no exceptions,
deadline overruns, invalid actions, folder warnings, or static-review flags.

The full fresh raw runs recorded **three candidate deadline misses for v6.0 and
six for v6.1**, with no policy exceptions. These counts must not be hidden by
the tactical comparison. Any fixture with either team's deadline miss was
repeated with exactly the same inputs; the first clean repeat was accepted
regardless of score, with a maximum of three retries. Five v6.0 fixtures and
eight v6.1 fixtures required repeats, including opponent deadline misses.
Every repeated fixture passed. **Raw and repeated-run aggregate scores were
identical**, including the per-opponent point totals.

Lower average computation time does not prove that deadline incidents are fixed.
The league screenshot's 18 combined exceptions/missed deadlines still lack an
error breakdown.

Focused checks passed for the keeper rule on both wings, short winning
collections, contested collections, clearance recovery, retained cover and
marking, mirrored receiver-aware wall passes, cooldown body obstructions, and
inelastic rebound geometry. On 100 recorded costly public positions, the wall
search stayed within its 12-forecast limit. Removing disabled experimental
branches changed no actions relative to the selected development variant.

The new keeper finding came from reproducible local replays. The live league
watch site was inaccessible from this environment; the previously supplied
videos were confirmed to use an older version.
