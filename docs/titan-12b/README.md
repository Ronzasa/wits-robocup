# TITAN-12B counterattack defence — qualification in progress

The active `my-team/team.py` remains the frozen v10.0 champion. All new
controllers are experimental and live on `titan-12b-counter-defence`.
No promotion or recommendation to replace the competition upload has been made.

The final verified-threat detector predicts actual incoming collections and at
most two legal imminent enemy releases after a loss. Designated cover and
emergency support have independent regressions/native checks before combination.
The combined controller passed 135 regressions and native invariants; kicks,
keeper/chaser commands, live receiver commands and pre-existing policy state
remain unchanged on shared observations. New assignments are temporary and keep
one emergency outlet, as explicitly authorized by the user.

The early heuristic cover and double-defence variants lost development points;
they are retained, not declared improvements. Several strict receiving-point
variants found no actionable positioning windows and remain unpromoted.

See [design and limitations](design.md), [initial registration](registration.json),
[seed registry](seed-registry.json), [final integration freeze](verified-integration-registration.json)
and [combination registration](combination-registration.json).
The next stages are the complete independent/combined development screen,
192-fixture expansion of its selected source, and one 768-pair fresh qualification
under every original promotion gate. No more tactical tuning is planned.
