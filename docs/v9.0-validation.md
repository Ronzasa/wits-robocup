## Reproduced decision error

In one reproduced v7 0–1 loss, three goal-directed kicks received positive
interception-time margins of **0.306, 0.084 and 0.216 seconds**. Opponent kicks
became visible only **2–3 observation ticks later**. At 20 Hz that is
0.10–0.15 seconds after the decision. The first own launch matched the engine;
the mismatch concerned the opponent's early response.

Players move and can kick at the pre-step ball before it advances. A shot check
that only evaluates later flight positions can miss this opportunity. The legacy
fallback could also retain a shot bonus after the planner found the route
threatened. The stored [public snapshots](../tests/fixtures/shot-contact-states.json)
are regression cases that were already examined; they are not fresh evidence.

## Isolated change

- Evaluate the first **three movement ticks (0.15 seconds)** using cached
  acceleration/momentum reach bounds and the speed cap. Check both a ready
  defender's kick at the pre-step ball and its body at the post-step ball.
  Cooldown removes a kick opportunity, not physical body obstruction. A defender
  can act before the next step would score, so the terminal check follows that
  opportunity.
- Apply the contact check to proposed shots. An attacking fallback shot that
  fails contact, interception or body criteria loses its safety bonus.
- A threatened shot can yield to an existing receiver-led pass or a controlled
  touch that passes the established recovery/interception test and the early
  contact check. A necessary fallback remains available when no better action
  qualifies; the change never requires an idle player to concede possession.
- Retain v7's off-ball formation, cover, marking, balanced counterpress, wall
  routes, keeper rules and restarts. Own-half clearance decisions retain their
  previous logic. Player speed, acceleration, kick impulse and ball speed caps
  are unchanged. No seeds, opponent names or hidden engine state select tactics.

The reach bounds approximate possible legal responses rather than predicting
which response the opponent will choose. Player interactions after the short
horizon remain approximate. This is not a guarantee that every shot gets through.
