# v9.1 strict interception challenger

Promoted: `my-team/team.py` and the workspace mirror now contain the tested challenger. The frozen v9.0 champion remains in `champions/v9.0/team.py`.

The pass-only challenger separates verified interception opportunities from the
heuristic fallback used by `_intercept()` for movement. This is a controlled local
comparison against v9.0, not a measured change in live league rank.

## Scope and physics

- `_verified_intercept()` returns the earliest witnessed contact in the relevant
  route, or `None`. An unavailable opponent interception is infeasible in that
  window, represented by infinity in comparisons; no heuristic ETA replaces it.
- `_pass_reachable()` first applies necessary speed/acceleration bounds, then
  requires a constructive legal movement witness: a constant input with legal
  intermediate speeds and no wall contact, or a stepped `_move` pursuit with
  damping, acceleration and the speed cap. Bounds alone cannot approve a pass.
- `_pass_path()` forecasts every simulation tick under public friction and wall
  restitution. It includes the carrier's first tick exactly once, the pre-step
  ball for legal kicks, both wall legs, and terminates at goal entry. This avoids
  missing collection windows between the general forecast's sparse samples.
- Use the documented full kick and body radii. Cooldown prevents kicks, never
  body obstruction. Current range alone is insufficient: a future kick must
  still be reachable after that tick's movement. Safety is a separate time margin.
- `_link_option()` / `_search_links()`, `_transition_release()`, `_endwall_link()`,
  `_bank_release()` and `_legacy_policy_kick()` all require the named receiver's
  verified opportunity before opponents. Rebound collection is checked after the
  bounce, while opponent checks cover the complete outbound and return route.
  Unsafe legacy passes fall back to existing retention or clearance choices.
- `act()` validates the actual corrected launch before creating either reception
  plan. It revalidates live trajectories, clears infeasible/contested plans, and
  refreshes bank targets rather than following stale rebound coordinates.

The heuristic `_intercept()`, `_eta()`, sparse movement trajectory, `_move()`,
cover, keeper reach, shot velocity/options, goal paths and shot-contact helpers
are unchanged and AST-checked. Keeper pass choices are subject to the same
required pass safety; no new goalkeeper or shooting system was introduced.

Movement witnesses assume collision-free player travel. They establish legal
possible motion, not unknown future player collisions, guaranteed ball control,
or which actions an opponent chooses. The solver is sufficient, not globally
complete: it can decline a physically possible opportunity it cannot witness.
Opponent searches cover the full route through collection and the safety/ranking
window; contacts more than 0.8 seconds after reception cannot affect capped ranks.

## Development, correction and freeze

The initial protocol registered eight styles, four development seeds and every
side/kickoff combination: 128 fixtures per source. The original strict prototype
added too much runtime. Shared tick conversion, cheap rejection bounds and bounded
ranking windows reduced the cost without intentional decision changes; actions
and plans matched across 1,800 shared native observations before boundary review.

Code review caught full-radius and initial-velocity witness edge cases after an
initial fresh prefix had started. Those incomplete prefixes were stopped,
retained separately, and excluded from all strength analysis and promotion.
Their scores were not inspected or used to choose changes. Ten new unused seeds
were registered; the original gate was retained. The corrected development source
earned **335** points, with 108/11/9 W/D/L
and 416–101 goals. The v9 champion earned 339 points.

Final source frozen at **2026-10-17 15:10:23 SAST**, before any corrected fresh fixture.
No fresh results were used to tune or replace the frozen challenger.
